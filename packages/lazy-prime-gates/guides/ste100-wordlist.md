# ASD-STE100 Issue 9 — Dictionary lookup

One line per dictionary entry, distilled from ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, Issue 9 (January 2025), Part 2.

- `WORD (pos) = approved: <meaning>` — the word is approved in STE with that part of speech and only that meaning.
- `word (pos) -> use: ALTERNATIVES` — the word is not approved; write one of the approved alternatives instead.
- A word approved as one part of speech is not approved as another (`test (v) -> use: DO A TEST` but `TEST (n)` is approved).
- Technical nouns (TN) and technical verbs (TV) from your project domain (API, endpoint, commit, branch, merge, token, cache, ...) are allowed under rules 1.5 and 1.12 even when this list gives an alternative for the general sense.

Grep this file when you are not sure a word is approved:
`grep -i '^word ' ste100-wordlist.md`
